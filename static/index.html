Music relay (AirPlay)
   
Signinator's AirPlay speaker takes the name of the speaker you pick here plus “ w/Art” (for example “Marantz Stereo w/Art”). Pick only that one on your phone: the sign shows the album art and the music plays on the speaker.

   
   
   
   
   
   
   

   
   
   

  

  
Sync test (lights vs. sound)
   
With the music stopped, this plays a soft tick every second on the relay speaker, and the sign flashes a white square when the music effects think the tick plays. Watch the sign, listen, and nudge until they happen together. If the flash comes first, press +.

Color

Calibrate
   

   
Origin: top-left pixel red, the one to its right green, the one below blue. If the real sign doesn't match, fix the mapping above (or WLED's 2D setup). Send a message to end the test.

  

  
Resting state
   
What the sign shows when nothing is playing. Pick it with ☾ Make resting next to Send in the Text, Effects or GIFs view.

   
   
No resting look saved yet.

   
   
  

  
Stereo power (Home Assistant)
   
Turns the stereo on when music starts and off after a while without music. Uses a Home Assistant long-lived access token (in Home Assistant: your profile → Security → Long-lived access tokens).

   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   

   

  

  
Home Assistant (MQTT)
   
Signinator shows up in Home Assistant as a device (MQTT discovery): power and brightness, message, effect, GIF, album art, song, and settings. Needs the MQTT integration (Mosquitto broker) in Home Assistant; log in with a Home Assistant user.
